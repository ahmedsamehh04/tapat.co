Feature tour

tap@

order @ your table

Every table gets a small NFC tag. Guests tap it with their phone and your restaurant's page opens in the browser: menu, ordering, rewards, feedback and a game. Nothing to download. You see every order live on your dashboard.

For your guests For you For your cashiers Contact us

How it works

one tap, no app

  1. Tap the tag

    The guest holds their phone to the tag on the table, or scans its QR code.

  2. Their table opens

    Your branding, your menu and their table number. They never pick a restaurant, branch or table.

  3. Order, earn, review

    They order from their seat, collect points and stamps, and leave feedback.

  4. You see it live

    The order lands on your dashboard with a sound alert, ready for the kitchen.

For your guests

the customer screen

Works on iPhone and Android, in light and dark mode, with your logo, banner, menu, links and rewards.

Every screen below is from Tap@G's, running now at tapat.co across its three branches: G's, G's Wellness Club and Garhy's.

Menu and ordering
  • Full PDF menu one tap away
  • Searchable menu with calories, protein, carbs and fat for each dish
  • Cart with quantities, VAT and the total
  • Order from the seat: the table is filled in and the order reaches you instantly
  • Daily order numbers such as #00007
Pay after eating
  • Live order status: "Still being prepared" until it's served
  • Ask for the bill with Card or Cash, and your staff are alerted
  • Personal promo codes from your WhatsApp offers
  • 14% VAT calculated and shown clearly
Loyalty
  • Points on every order
  • Rewards: free items or a % off the next order
  • Stamp card: a full card puts the free item in the cart automatically
  • Personal QR code for staff to scan
Engagement
  • Anonymous feedback with 1 to 5 stars and a comment
  • Google review in one tap
  • Social links: Instagram, Facebook and TikTok
  • BlockBomb, a puzzle game for while the food is on its way
Account
  • Sign up and log in with email or phone, or Google on the website
  • Profile with birthday, gender and phone
  • Forgot password by email
  • WhatsApp offers: opt in, and opt out at any time
Privacy by consent
  • You see a short name like "Sara M.", age and gender only if the guest allows it
  • Their email is never shared with you
  • Terms and Privacy Policy accepted at sign-up

For you, the owner

the admin dashboard

A private login for the owner. Manage one branch or several, and several restaurants, from the same account.

Overview

your numbers at a glance

  • Revenue with and without VAT, orders and customers
  • Filter by branch and by today, this week, this month or your own dates
  • Revenue chart, top 5 best sellers and recent orders
Insights

know what sells, and when

  • This period against the last one
  • Busiest hours and days
  • Cash against card and a branch comparison
  • Revenue by category, best and slowest sellers, top tables
Menu control

sold out in one tap

  • Sold out per branch, live for guests at once
  • Edit names, prices, categories, descriptions and nutrition
  • Add, hide or delete dishes
  • Choose the stamp card's size and free item
Feedback

hear it first

  • Average rating and the star breakdown
  • Low-rating alerts that need a look
  • Every comment with its branch, table and time
Customers and marketing

bring them back

  • Members, gender split and age groups
  • Customer list with orders and spend for each person
  • WhatsApp offers: a suggested discount on their favourite dish with a one-time personal promo code
Inventory

stock that counts itself

  • Stock per branch with restock, stock-take and low-stock warnings
  • Recipes: how much of each ingredient one dish uses
  • Stock goes down by itself as orders are prepared
  • Dishes sell out automatically when an ingredient runs out or expires
Daily sales report

close the day in one print

  • Payments by cash and card, returns and net payments
  • Products mix by category with quantities and amounts
  • Print on the 80 mm receipt printer or download as PDF
Invoices

suppliers and buyers, kept apart

  • Payables to suppliers and receivables from buyers
  • Record payments and see what's outstanding and overdue
  • Print or download A4 invoices as PDF
Live orders

The kitchen's screen, in real time

  • New orders appear instantly with how long each has waited
  • One tap moves an order on: New, Preparing, Ready, Served, or Cancel
  • Bills: tables asking for the bill with Card or Cash and the total, a Paid button and Print receipt on an 80 mm printer or as a PDF
  • Alerts for new orders and bill requests with sound, vibration and browser notifications, on every staff screen
Order history
  • Every order, as a list or grouped by customer
  • Search and filters
Employees
  • Cashier accounts for each branch
  • Deactivate one and they're signed out immediately
  • Password reset by email
Loyalty scanner
  • Scan a guest's QR with the camera to add a stamp
Several branches
  • One owner login for every branch
  • Filter by branch on every screen
