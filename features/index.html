tap@

order @ your table

Every table gets a small NFC tag. Guests tap it with their phone and your restaurant's page opens in the browser: menu, ordering, rewards, feedback and a game. Nothing to download. You see every order live on your dashboard.

How it works

one tap, no app

  1. Tap the tag

    The guest holds their phone to the tag on the table, or scans its QR code.

  2. Their table opens

    Your branding, your menu and their table number. They never pick a restaurant, branch or table.

  3. Order, earn, review

    They order from their seat, collect points and stamps, and leave feedback.

  4. You see it live

    The order lands on your dashboard with a sound alert, ready for the kitchen.

For your guests

the customer screen

Works on iPhone and Android, in light and dark mode, with your logo, banner, menu, links and rewards.

Every screen on this page is from tap@, our demo café with two branches, Alexandria and Cairo. Scroll inside a screen to see all of it.

arrive and browse

  • Tap to open: the table is already known, no app or sign-up needed
  • Your full PDF menu and a searchable menu to order from

order, then pay after eating

  • Cart with VAT, the total and the points they'll earn
  • Order from the seat: it reaches the kitchen instantly
  • Ask for the bill with Card or Cash, plus personal promo codes

points, stamps and rewards

  • Points on every order, rewards from a free cookie to 20% off
  • Stamp card: a full card puts the free item in the cart
  • Personal QR code for staff to scan

feedback and fun

  • Anonymous feedback with stars and a comment, straight to you
  • Google review, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok in one tap
  • BlockBomb, a puzzle game for while the food is on its way

accounts and privacy

  • Sign up and log in with email or phone, or Google on the web
  • Privacy by consent: you see a short name, age and gender only if they allow it, never their email
  • WhatsApp offers only for guests who opt in
