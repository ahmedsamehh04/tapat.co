how it works

one tag. four steps.

  1. a tag on every table

    Each tag holds a link that already knows your restaurant, branch and table number.

  2. guests tap their phone

    The page opens in the browser on iPhone and Android. There is no app to download.

  3. they order from their seat

    Menu, cart and checkout, with the table already filled in. Cash or card, paid at the table.

  4. you see it live

    The order reaches your dashboard in real time, and you move it along as the kitchen works.

questions

good to know.

do guests need an app?

No. The tag opens a web page in the phone's browser, on iPhone and Android.

how do guests pay?

At the table, as they do today. They choose cash or card when they order, so your staff know what to bring.

what do I need in the restaurant?

One NFC tag per table, and a phone, tablet or computer that stays on your dashboard.

what if a phone has no NFC?

Each table also has a link that opens the same page, so every guest can order.

what do I learn about my guests?

Only what they agree to share: age, gender and a shortened name. Their email and phone number are never shared with restaurants.

can I run more than one branch?

Yes. Each branch has its own tables, sold-out items and numbers, under one login.
